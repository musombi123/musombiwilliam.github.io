03 — PRODUCTS & ENGINEERING

RevelaCode — an integrated digital platform

FLAGSHIP AI PRODUCT

RevelaAI — an intelligent, multi-purpose workspace

RevelaAI is the AI workspace within the RevelaCode platform. It is designed to help people research, learn, create, analyse information and work across different subject areas from one conversational interface.

  • Conversational AI — context-aware chat for questions, explanations, planning and problem-solving.
  • Research & knowledge support — structured exploration across scripture, education, science, technology and general topics.
  • Document & file workflows — assistance with reading, extracting, summarising and working with supplied information.
  • Developer assistance — code explanation, debugging support, technical planning and IDE-oriented workflows.
  • Multimodal direction — voice, image understanding and visual creation capabilities as supported by the active model and release.
  • Task-oriented tools — specialised actions for analysis, writing, data presentation and hub-specific workflows.
  • Personal workspace — conversation history, preferences and a focused interface for continuing work.

AI responses and tool availability depend on the configured model, permissions, subscription and current release. Model providers are accessed through a backend integration layer rather than exposing provider credentials in the browser.

THE PLATFORM

One shared foundation. Purpose-built hubs.

RevelaCode follows a modular architecture: shared identity, access control and platform services support focused workspaces for different sectors. Each hub has its own workflows and permissions rather than forcing every user into one generic application.

01 / EDUCATION

Elimu Hub

A school operations and learning-management workspace designed to bring administrative and academic processes into one coordinated system.

  • School administration — school profiles, staff roles, learner records and controlled access.
  • Finance operations — fee tracking, voteheads, balances, payment records and reconciliation workflows.
  • Academic operations — classes, subjects, attendance, assessment and learner progress.
  • People & communication — staff coordination, parent-facing communication and school notices.
  • Operational oversight — role-specific workspaces for owners, principals, registrars, teachers and bursars.

The objective is a coherent school-management workflow with financial and academic responsibilities separated by role.

02 / AGRICULTURE

Shamba Hub

A digital farm-management environment focused on giving farmers practical tools to organise farm activity and make better-informed decisions.

  • Farmer & farm profiles — onboarding, verification and management of multiple farms.
  • Farm operations — crop records, planting activity, farm observations and production planning.
  • Crop intelligence — guided crop, pest and disease information, with image-assisted analysis where available.
  • Weather context — location-aware weather information to support planning when data services are available.
  • Market connections — a foundation for produce listings, marketplace activity and farmer services.

Recommendations are decision-support information; field verification and qualified agricultural advice remain important.

03 / BUSINESS OPERATIONS

Biashara OS

A configurable operating environment for shops, service businesses, factories and other organisations that need visibility across daily operations.

  • Sales & orders — transaction workflows, order tracking and customer-facing operations.
  • Inventory & stores — stock movement, product records and store accountability.
  • Finance — income, expenses, operational records and management reporting.
  • People & production — team responsibilities, HR workflows and factory-floor coordination.
  • Configurable roles — access designed around owners, managers, finance, stores, sales and production teams.

The platform direction is tenant-aware: different organisations can use tailored workflows on a common application foundation.

AI CAPABILITY & ENGINEERING STACK

How the intelligence layer is organised

RevelaAI is more than a chat window. Its design separates the user experience, model connectivity, tool execution and platform permissions so capabilities can be extended without putting secrets or privileged operations in the client.

AI interaction tools

  • Conversational assistant and contextual follow-up.
  • Writing assistance and text humanisation workflows.
  • Document and file understanding.
  • Code explanation, debugging and development assistance.
  • Research support and structured answer generation.
  • Data analysis with tables, summaries and chart-oriented outputs.
  • Image understanding and visual-generation workflows where enabled.
  • Voice interaction and speech-related workflows where enabled.

Tool orchestration

  • Hub-aware routing for education, agriculture, business, faith and general tasks.
  • Backend-mediated model and provider integration.
  • Permission checks and plan-aware access to specialised tools.
  • Structured tool requests and result handling.
  • Conversation context and workspace continuity.
  • Fallback behaviour for constrained connectivity or unavailable tools.

Application architecture

  • React and Vite-based web application interfaces.
  • Separate backend services for the core platform and AI workloads.

Access across devices

The product strategy is multi-channel: responsive web experiences, an Android application and a Windows desktop direction, with mobile-first workflows and offline-aware data handling where supported.

Shared platform principles

Common identity, role-based access, modular services, clear data ownership, secure API boundaries and consistent user experience across the ecosystem.

05 — DIGITAL INVESTIGATION

Digital forensics, reverse engineering & defensive security

A disciplined engineering track focused on evidence preservation, data recovery methods, software behaviour and authorized security validation.

I am building practical capability to investigate digital evidence, recover data where technically possible, understand software and binary behaviour, and validate security weaknesses only in systems for which clear authorization has been granted.

The work combines Python-based analysis, low-level programming, file-system knowledge, evidence preservation, reverse-engineering frameworks and standards-led application security testing. This is an active learning and portfolio track—not a claim of certification or a guarantee that every damaged device or suspected vulnerability can be resolved.

Featured engineering build: RevelaAI Portable Field Kit v0.2.0 — a local utility layer for SHA-256 evidence inventories, integrity verification, hash-linked custody records, static PE/ELF/APK inspection and defensive source checks.

Investigation principles

  • 01 Authorization, scope and evidence provenance
  • 02 Preserve originals; analyze verified working copies
  • 03 SHA-256 integrity records and reproducible methods
  • 04 Static analysis before controlled lab execution
  • 05 Findings separated from assumptions and limitations
